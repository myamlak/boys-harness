## 4. What fails or is limited

- **BRASIL's polynomial mode cannot supply the stored polynomial arm.** It computes the minimax polynomial correctly (its internal error agrees with Remez to about 1e-4 relative at p5 degrees 14-18 and to 8% at degree 20; its internal error is sampled, not the true supremum), but the barycentric-to-monomial conversion at these degrees is numerically unstable. At p5 degree 14, its internal minimax error is 1.656e-09, but the stored monomial conversion has max |coefficient| 8.700e+07 and delivered relative error 6.504e+08; at degree 16 the corresponding numbers are 2.636e+07 and 1.971e+08. The measurement is in `validation_brasil_polynomial_conversion`; it is used only as an independent quality reference, not for the stored arm.
- **The float64 Remez exchange can stop early at high degree** (`alternation failed` in the scan rows). The 60-digit linear polish and the high-precision exchange then certify the result: the equioscillation spread is at machine precision, and the dense-grid maximum equals the claimed minimax error to within 2x. Where the below-degree error was within 1.6x the target, the high-precision recheck settled it; one count moved (p0, rel:1e-14, 9 -> 8).
- **The discrete-minimax linear program is reliable only when the minimax error is above about 1e-9**; below that HiGHS's feasibility tolerance dominates and the returned objective collapses. It is a cross-check, not the production solver.
- **Seven fits carry at least one strict mismatch between the stored witness and the recorded delivered error or guard.** All are non-converged BRASIL fits; the delivered differences are at most 1.8e-15 absolute and the guard differs by at most 6e-17. The root isolation uses the stored denominator, whose guard matches the record within 1e-12 in every one of the 98 fits.
- **No denominator root on [-1,1], and no off-grid pole, was found in any of the 98 accepted fits.** This is a finite record, not a proof for fits outside it; the W=32 control's clean result does not generalise by assumption, only by the 98-fit check reported here.
