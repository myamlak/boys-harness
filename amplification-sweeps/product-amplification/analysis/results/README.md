# Regenerated prediction records

These three records are the prediction records the tables in `../tables/` are
made from. They were generated by `sweeps/amplification_form/analyze.py` in
this directory, which carries the exponential term in the accumulated-arithmetic
model, from the deposited records at
`../../v5.1.1-9353/sweeps/amplification_form/`.

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `prediction.csv` | the 132 band-metric-order rows at the scored margin |
| `prediction_scan.csv` | the score over the margin ladder |
| `prediction_record.csv` | the same rows with the argument at which each band maximum is attained |

`../README.md` records that three tables (`endtoend.tex`, `frontier.tex`,
`prediction-record.tex`) changed after the check when `analyze.py` gained the
exponential term. The v5.1.1-9353 prediction records predate that term and give
67 commitments and 65 abstentions, five at margin two; these regenerated records
give 65 commitments and 67 abstentions, four at margin two, and are the ones the
tables reproduce.
