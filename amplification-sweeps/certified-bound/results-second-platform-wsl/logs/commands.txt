Result in one paragraph
-----------------------
The full 55,176-entry comparison runs on the second platform and reproduces
the deposited record: 0 bound violations, worst |r|/C 0.71875 at
(A1, 0, 24, ship), 264/264 population bands inside, all 132 band comparisons
overlap (the band_cert.csv file is byte-identical), 65/65 commitments overlap,
49 truncation-agreeing and 16 truncation-opposite, loser C/|t| medians 83.29
over the 59 recursed losers and 13.31 over the six direct losers, required
factor median 85.23 over the 49 agreeing comparisons, range 9.00 to 3937.69.
The delivered doubles differ from the Windows/MSVC record at 70 of 55,176
entries (all recursed, five arguments, largest relative difference 9.4e-10),
every one traceable to a one-ulp glibc-versus-UCRT exponential difference;
all 27,588 direct entries are bit-identical.  two per_order at_x rows move
(A1 absolute orders 18 and 29, both abstentions), which leaves the commitment
cell set, harness_cells.csv, band_cert.csv and model_cells.csv byte-identical
to the deposited record.
