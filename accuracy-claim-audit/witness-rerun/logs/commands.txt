Note on the audit's build command
---------------------------------
accuracy-claim-audit/README.md describes the crlibm build as
non-double-extended but its listed configure line does not pass
--disable-double-extended.  configure.ac gives the double-extended branch
as the default, so on this host the flag is required to obtain the
documented configuration.  csh_fast.c, the sinh source, has no
double-extended conditional; the flag selects log.c rather than log-de.c.
